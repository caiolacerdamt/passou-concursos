The camera moves forward down the corridor in one single continuous dolly, travelling straight ahead towards the bright opening at the far end. The move is slow, smooth, steady and perfectly constant in speed from the first frame to the last, like a camera on a dolly track. The camera never tilts, never turns, never rotates and never rises: it only travels forward along the centre of the corridor.

As the camera advances, the paper walls on the left and on the right slide past on both sides as parallax, and the bright opening at the far end grows steadily larger in the frame. By the end of the clip the camera has arrived close to the opening and the small bright room is clearly visible, but the camera has not entered it.

Nothing in the scene changes, transforms, moves or animates by itself. The paper walls stay solid and still. The green blocks and the chair inside the bright room stay exactly as they are, in the same places, and only grow larger because the camera is approaching them. The left wall stays plain, even and low in contrast for the whole clip.

Nothing enters the frame and nothing leaves the frame. No person, no human figure and no hand appears at any point. One single continuous take. No cuts, no camera shake, no zoom snap, no direction change, no scene change. No text, no letters, no numbers and no logos appear at any point.
